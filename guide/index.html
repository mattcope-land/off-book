    How to use Learn Lines

    Learn your lines with a scene partner that reads your cues out loud and listens to you say your lines.

    On this page

      🎭 Add your show

      Tap + Add a show and choose how:

      • From the script: upload the script as a PDF or take photos of the pages. Learn Lines reads the whole script, then asks who you play. Tick every name your character goes by (like PUCK and ROBIN), plus ALL or CHORUS if you speak or sing with the group. You need the passcode for this, but only once.
      • From a spreadsheet: a CSV file with Cue and Line columns, and optionally Scene.
      • Type my lines: enter each cue (the line just before yours) and your line.
      🔗 Got a link from your director or a castmate? Just open it, tap your character, and you're ready. No passcode needed. Using Learn Lines from your home screen? Tap + Add a show → From a share link and paste the link (or type its code) instead.
      ↑ Back to top

      ▶️ Practice

      Open your show and tap Practice. Pick a scene (or all of them) and how:

      • In order: your lines in script order. Best when you're starting out.
      • Shuffle: mixed up, so you know every line without the one before it.
      • Tricky ones: only the lines you don't know yet.

      On each card you'll see the cue, and the lines before it for context. Then just follow the big button:

      • 🔊 Hear the cue, then 🎤 Say my line. Learn Lines listens, shows your line, and underlines any words you missed.
      • Hands-free (in ⚙️ Settings, on by default) does it all for you: it plays the cue, listens, and moves on when you get it right. Perfect for running lines while you walk around.
      • No microphone? Say your line out loud, tap 👁 Show, then tap ✓ Got it or ✗ Missed it.
      • Stuck? 💡 Hint shows the first letter of each word. Tap More to reveal one word at a time.
      • If it heard you wrong, tap I got it or Try again under the button.
      ⌨️ On a laptop: Space is the big button, ← → go back and forward, H is a hint, S shows your line, and 1 / 2 mean missed / got it.

      📈 Getting off book

      • Get a line right 3 times in a row without a hint and it's known. Miss it and it goes back to learning.
      • Your show shows how much you're off book, scene by scene. Tap a scene to practice just that one.
      • Lines you miss in Shuffle or Tricky ones come back a few cards later.
      • Practice every day to build your 🔥 streak. Finish a scene and you'll know about it. 🎉

      🗣️ Voices

      • Cues are read by natural voices, with a different voice for each character. They need the internet the first time, then work offline.
      • Choose a different voice or the cue speed in ⚙️ Settings during practice. The voices built into your device work offline and don't need anything; the best ones are marked ★.
      • On iPhone and iPad you can download better device voices: Settings → Accessibility → Spoken Content → Voices → English, then pick one marked Enhanced or Premium.

      🔗 Share with your cast

      Open a show and tap Share with cast to get a link. Everyone who opens it picks their own character and gets their own lines, cues and voices. The same link moves the show to your other phone or tablet.

      Under the link there's also a short code, like AbC1-dEf2-GhI3, that people can type in instead: under + Add a show → From a share link. Capital and small letters matter.

      ↑ Back to top

      📲 Put it on your home screen

      • iPhone / iPad (Safari): tap Share ⬆︎, then Add to Home Screen.
      • Android (Chrome): tap Install when Learn Lines offers it, or ⋮ → Install app.
      • Computer (Chrome / Edge): click the install icon at the end of the address bar.
      On iPhone and iPad, the home-screen app keeps its own separate copy of your shows. Links always open in Safari, so to move a show over: open it in Safari, tap Share with cast, copy the link, then in the home-screen app tap + Add a show → From a share link and paste it.
      ↑ Back to top

      🔒 Your data

      • Your shows and progress are saved on this device. There are no accounts.
      • Clearing your browser's data deletes them, so keep your share link, or use Edit script → Export CSV for a backup.
      • Importing a script sends it to an AI service (Fireworks AI) to find the lines. Sharing stores the script so your cast's links work. Natural voices send the cue text to Cloudflare to be read aloud.
      • 🎤 checking uses your browser's speech recognition, which may send your voice to Apple or Google to be transcribed. Learn Lines doesn't record or keep it.
