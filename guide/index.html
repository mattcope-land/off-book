OFF BOOK

Actor's Line Learner: User Guide

Off Book is a digital rehearsal tool designed to help actors memorize scripts through cue-based flashcards and audio synthesis.

1

Production Setup

To begin, you must define the production you are working on.

  • Create a Production: From the home screen, click + Add.
  • Details: Enter the play title and the specific role you are rehearsing.
  • Management: You can return to the home screen at any time by clicking the "Off Book" header.
2

Building the Script

The app uses a "Cue and Response" system to simulate a scene partner.

  • Adding Lines: Enter the Cue (the line immediately preceding yours) and Your Line. Click Add Line to save the pair.
  • Scene Organization: Use the Scene Heading field to create organizational breaks (e.g., "Act 1, Scene 2").
  • Ordering: If lines are entered out of sequence, use the handle (⠿) to drag and drop them into the correct order.
  • Bulk Import: You may use the Import CSV feature to upload a spreadsheet of lines. Ensure your CSV has headers for "Cue" and "Line."
3

Practice Modes

Once your script is populated, select a rehearsal method from the top navigation:

  • In Order: Recommended for early memorization. You can choose to practice the entire script or a specific scene.
  • Random: Recommended for advanced mastery to ensure you know your lines regardless of context.
4

The Flashcard Interface

  • Prompt: The "Cue" is displayed first.
  • Audio: Click 🔊 Listen to hear the cue read aloud by the text-to-speech engine.
  • Verification: Recite your line, then click the Cue text to reveal the correct response and verify your accuracy.
  • Navigation: Use the Next and Previous buttons to move through the script.
5

Settings & Customization

Access the ⚙️ Settings menu during practice to optimize your session:

  • Voice Engine: Select from available system voices to differentiate the cue's tone.
  • Playback Speed: Adjust the "Rate" slider to practice at different performance tempos.
  • Autoplay: Enable "Auto-play cue" to have the app automatically speak the next cue as soon as you advance the card.
  • Interface: Use the sun/moon button in the header to switch between light and dark modes.
6

Data Policy

  • Data is stored locally in your browser. There are no accounts or logins.
  • If you clear browser data, you will lose your saved lines. There is no sync between devices.
  • Export a CSV backup frequently to save your work safely outside the browser.
  • Note: Scene breaks do not currently export to CSV. I'll try to add that in a future update.
