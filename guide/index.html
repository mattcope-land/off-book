LEARN LINES

Actor's Line Learner: User Guide

Learn Lines is a digital rehearsal tool designed to help actors memorize scripts through cue-based flashcards and audio synthesis.

1

Production Setup

To begin, you must define the production you are working on.

  • Create a Production: From the home screen, click + Add.
  • Details: Enter the play title and the specific role you are rehearsing.
  • Management: You can return to the home screen at any time by clicking the "LEARN LINES" header.
2

Building the Script

The app uses a "Cue and Response" system to simulate a scene partner.

  • Adding Lines: Enter the Cue (the line immediately preceding yours) and Your Line. Click Add Line to save the pair. If you speak first, leave the cue blank.
  • Editing: Tap any line or scene heading in the Script Structure list to edit it.
  • Scene Organization: Use the Scene Heading field to create organizational breaks (e.g., "Act 1, Scene 2").
  • Ordering: Press and drag the handle (⠿) to move lines into the correct order. This works with a finger on phones and tablets too.
  • Bulk Import: Use the Import CSV feature to upload a spreadsheet with the columns Cue and Line. You can add an optional third column, Scene, with the scene each line belongs to (e.g. "Act 1, Scene 2"); a scene break is added wherever the scene changes. The easiest way to get a template is to click Export CSV.
  • Import from Script: Click ✨ Import from Script and upload the script as a PDF, photos of the pages, or a text file. Enter your character's name as it appears in the script (plus any other names they go by) and the passcode you were given. AI finds your lines and the cue before each one, and shows them to you to check and fix before they're added. Always read them over: AI can make mistakes, especially with photos.
3

Practice Modes

Once your script is populated, select a rehearsal method from the top navigation:

  • In Order: Recommended for early memorization. You can choose to practice the entire script or a specific scene.
  • Random: Recommended for advanced mastery to ensure you know your lines regardless of context.
  • Tricky Lines: Only the lines you don't know yet. Use this once you've been through the script a few times.
4

The Flashcard Interface

  • Prompt: The "Cue" is displayed first.
  • Audio: Click 🔊 Listen to hear the cue read aloud by the text-to-speech engine.
  • Verification: Recite your line, then tap the cue or 👁 Show Line to reveal the correct response and verify your accuracy.
  • Say It: Tap 🎤 Say It and say your line out loud. The app listens, reveals your line, and marks any words you missed with a red underline. It stops by itself once you've said every word, or tap ⏹ Done. The first time, your browser will ask for permission to use the microphone. (Works in Chrome, Edge and Safari; the button is hidden in browsers that don't support it.)
  • Hints: Stuck? Tap 💡 Hint to see the first letter of each word of your line. Tap 💡 More to reveal one more word at a time.
  • Grading: After you see your line, tap ✓ Got it or ✗ Missed it to move on. 🎤 Say It grades for you (a perfect line counts as got it). If it misheard you, tap the right button instead.
  • Navigation: Use the Next and Previous buttons to move through the script. At the end you'll see how you did, with the option to practice just the lines you missed.
5

Tracking Progress

Learn Lines remembers how you do on every line, shown by the dot next to it:

  • Grey (new): You haven't practiced it yet.
  • Yellow (learning): You're working on it.
  • Green (known): You've got it right 3 times in a row without a hint. Missing it again turns it back to yellow.
  • In Random and Tricky Lines practice, a line you miss comes back again a few cards later.
  • Editing a line resets its progress. To start over on the whole production, tap Reset progress.
6

Settings & Customization

Access the ⚙️ Settings menu during practice to optimize your session:

  • Natural Voices: The voices at the top of the list sound much more like a real scene partner. They need an internet connection and the same passcode as Import from Script. Each cue is saved on your device the first time it plays, so it's instant after that. Tap ⬇️ Download all cues for offline to get every cue ready before a rehearsal with no signal.
  • Device Voices: The voices built into your phone or computer work offline and need no passcode. The best ones are marked ★. On iPhone and iPad you can download better ones: go to Settings → Accessibility → Spoken Content → Voices → English and download a voice marked Enhanced or Premium. In Microsoft Edge, the voices marked Natural are very good.
  • Playback Speed: Adjust the "Rate" slider to practice at different performance tempos.
  • Autoplay: Enable "Auto-play cue" to have the app automatically speak the next cue as soon as you advance the card.
  • Scene Partner Mode: Turn on "Listen for my line after the cue plays" as well, and the app will read the cue and then start listening for your line automatically. Just tap Next between lines.
7

Install on Your Phone

Learn Lines can be installed like an app, with its own icon, and works without an internet connection once installed.

  • iPhone / iPad (Safari): Tap the Share button, then Add to Home Screen.
  • Android (Chrome): Tap the ⋮ menu, then Install app or Add to Home screen.
  • Computer (Chrome / Edge): Click the install icon at the right end of the address bar.
  • Important on iPhone and iPad: the installed app keeps its own separate copy of your data. Before installing, Export CSV for each production in Safari, then Import CSV into the installed app.
  • Updates: After an update is released, it appears the next time you open the app.
8

Data Policy

  • Data is stored locally in your browser. There are no accounts or logins.
  • You can close the browser, open the app in a new tab, or navigate away from the page and your data will be saved.
  • But, if you clear browser data, you will lose your saved lines. There is no sync between devices.
  • Export a CSV backup frequently to save your work safely outside the browser. The backup includes your scene breaks, but not your progress.
  • Say It uses your browser's built-in speech recognition, which may send your voice to Apple or Google to be transcribed. Nothing is recorded or saved by Learn Lines.
  • Import from Script sends the script pages you choose to an AI service (Fireworks AI) to find your lines. Learn Lines doesn't keep a copy.
  • Natural voices send the cue text to Cloudflare to be read aloud. The audio is saved so each cue only has to be created once.
